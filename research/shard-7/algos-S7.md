# Shard 7 — Indigenous American, African, Pacific, and Arctic record structures

Ninety rank and index rules for Vestige. Forty-five are a single documented record structure. Forty-five fire only when two of those structures both hold. The base is `e2ecd1da037974c1835f563ad8b046f4a4ca3629` on `perf/c2-step33-triangulation`. Nothing here edits the engine.

Open-Domain ordering cannot pass 35/92. The attached scoreboard has Turn@1 at 18 and Turn@5 at 35; Step 33 on this branch moves Turn@1 to 19 and leaves Turn@5 at 35. Pool@50 is 63 (C2_STEP33_LOG). Reaching 83/92 means recalling at least 20 questions that are outside pool@50 and then seating those, plus the 63, at slot 1. Preference ordering cannot pass 14/39. The broader pool holds 30, and 90% of 39 is 36, so at least 6 of the 9 questions with no pool hit have to be retrieved. Step 33 does not run on user/assistant transcripts, so it does not move BEAM.

Every rule uses integer or ordinal comparisons, `DetHashMap` / `DetHashSet`, and closed-class or orthographic shape. None reads `TurnIx.body` on the rank path, none adds a domain word list, and none writes `rsb_seat_behind`.
